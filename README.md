### 💡 The Core Reality

For a long time, I have wanted to just be my authentic self in my videos here. My English is still a work in progress. I can easily communicate with founders, investors, and candidates using my pre-prepared frameworks in English. 

But when it comes to videos, I want to share my actual flow of thoughts and insights. Trying to translate it in my head while filming just changes the energy - **I want to give you the pure clarity of my reflections.**

So, I did a little tech *“magic”* and created this video. It is me on screen, but the subtitles and the voiceover are fully in English, powered by some great modern AI tools. 

### 🎯 How I Work

This is exactly how I plan to work with founders who choose a **calculated path in hiring**. The ones who want to see first: 
1. Who do they *actually* need to solve specific business tasks?
2. Define those tasks with absolute clarity.
3. And *only then* look for the exact person to solve them.

I want to work with founders in close connection, supporting them and going deep into every single request from my dear clients. **NOT for the money or reputation**, but from a genuine desire to do something good and meaningful. And I will put all my effort into it.
